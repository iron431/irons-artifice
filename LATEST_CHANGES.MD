### Additions
- Added Pirate Drowned Ambushes
  - When looting a water themed treasure chest, there is a 25% chance (configurable) to spawn a Drowned Pirate Ambush at a nearby valid water spawnpoint
    - (Large Ruins Chest, Buried Treasure, Shipwreck Treasure)
  - Drowneds spawn with guns, pirate hats, and can ride zombie nautilus
  - Have extra loot, and can drop their equipment
- Added Japanese Localization, thanks to Vivi Love
- Added Ukrainian and Russian Localization, thanks to Starman
- Added Tartar localization, thanks to Kerpichikken

### Changes
- Reworked Early Game Progression
  - A single Blackpowder can now be crafted from two charcoal
  - Simple Mechanical Components no longer require Redstone to craft
  - Mechanical Components now require Redstone to craft
  - The goal is that, by moving the requirement of Redstone to later, primitive guns and limited ammo can be acquired much earlier in a playthrough
- Six-Shooter now requries only one Mechanical Component to craft, instead of two
- Buffed Flintlock Reload speed from 3s to 2s
- Nerfed Incendiary Tip
  - +15% damage reduced to +10%
  - 5 burn seconds reduced to 4 seconds
- Buffed Enchanted Bullet
  - -10% modifier increased to -12.5%

### Fixes
- Implemented Scope workaround to bypass Waystone's breaking of FOV modifiers
